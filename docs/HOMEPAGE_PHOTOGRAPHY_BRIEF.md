# Sproutflow Homepage Photography Brief

## Goal

Create an honest visual library that makes Sproutflow feel like a founder-led working studio. The images should show the person, process, and real businesses behind the finished digital work.

## Required Shots

- Ben working at a desk with an actual client project visible but no private information exposed
- An informal environmental portrait with natural window light
- Discovery notes, sketches, and marked-up page structures in progress
- Responsive testing on a phone, tablet, and laptop
- Close details of a keyboard, notebook, printed layout, and hand-drawn workflow
- Ben in a recognizable New Orleans setting without relying on tourist landmarks
- Client spaces, products, tools, or environments with written permission
- A finished site shown in use by the business owner or staff

## Art Direction

- Natural light, honest texture, and true-to-life color
- Mix wider environmental frames with close process details
- Leave negative space for responsive crops and short captions
- Photograph real working moments rather than staged pointing or handshakes
- Keep wardrobe and locations consistent with the grounded green, cream, and charcoal brand palette

## Avoid

- Generic plants used as a metaphor for growth
- Empty laptop mockups, fake teams, or rented office settings
- Heavy color grading, dramatic studio shadows, or luxury-agency posing
- Visible customer data, passwords, private notes, or unapproved client material

## Delivery

- Provide landscape 3:2, portrait 4:5, and wide 16:9 crops where possible
- Export high-resolution originals plus web-ready AVIF or WebP versions
- Record subject, location, usage permission, and descriptive alt-text notes for every final image
